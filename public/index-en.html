Why is the Paraná-Paraguay
so strategic?

It is the logistics backbone of Mercosur. Without it, commodity transport costs would multiply 5x compared to land freight.

5x cheaper than trucks

River transport via the Paraná-Paraguay reduces logistics costs by up to 80% compared to road transport. A 16-barge formation equals 960 trucks.

Minimal carbon footprint

River transport emits 5 times less CO₂ per ton/km than trucks. Companies operating on the waterway lead the transition to sustainable logistics.

Unstoppable growth

Traffic on the Paraná-Paraguay grows 8% annually. Investments in dredging, signaling, and private terminals exceed USD 2.000M planned until 2030.
